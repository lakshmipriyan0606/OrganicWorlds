The Art of Clean-Burning Botanical Candles

At Earth & Essence, every candle is conceived as a tactile sculpture for the senses. We reject conventional paraffin waxes in favor of a proprietary blend of sustainably cultivated soy and coconut waxes that burn cleaner, slower, and cooler, ensuring zero petroleum soot buildup in your living spaces.

Our fragrance formulation process mirrors fine perfumery. By working directly with master botanists across the Pacific Northwest and Mediterranean, we extract rare aromatic resins, wild-harvested mosses, and sun-cured woods to create complex scent profiles that evolve gracefully as the wax pool deepens.

Each candle features an unbleached 100% organic cotton or FSC-certified wood wick designed to produce a gentle, meditative crackle reminiscent of an open hearth. Hand-poured in small batches within our studio, our vessels are chosen for their reusable durability—transforming effortlessly into planters, desk organizers, or heirloom containers once the wax has naturally depleted.

Whether you seek the grounding, earthy aroma of Cedarwood & Moss to anchor your evening meditation, or the crisp, clarifying notes of White Pine & Smoke to invigorate a workspace, our collection invites you to curate sanctuary within the everyday.
