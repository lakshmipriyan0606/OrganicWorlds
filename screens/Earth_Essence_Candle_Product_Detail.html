Living with Your Candle

Hand-poured natural waxes require thoughtful attention during their initial lighting to preserve the wick health, memory pool, and vessel life.

verified

The Earth & Essence Promise

If your candle tunnels or does not burn cleanly to the outer stoneware rim, contact our master pourer for an immediate replacement.

The Memory Pool: On your very first lighting, allow the candle to burn continuously for 3 to 4 hours until the melt pool reaches the outer stoneware perimeter. Soy and beeswax maintain a wax memory; extinguishing prematurely may create tunneling that diminishes future fragrance projection.

Always trim the braided hemp wick to exactly 5mm (1/4 inch) prior to every subsequent lighting to prevent mushrooming, smoking, or excessive flame flicker. Use a wick snuffer rather than blowing directly over the wax to preserve delicate top resin notes.

We decline synthetic stabilizers, phthalates, parabens, and chemical scent boosters. Our recipe comprises:

  • Wild Indian Soy Wax: Harvested from sustainably managed non-GMO agricultural rotations.
  • Apiary Cappings Beeswax: Gently gathered during honeycomb uncapping by ethical bee conservationists.
  • Kyoto Hinoki Distillate: Responsibly gathered timber trimmings from Nagano and Kyoto reforested groves.
  • Braided Hemp Wick: Pure unbleached natural organic hemp twisted with paper threads.

When approximately 1/2 inch of wax remains, do not light again. Fill the vessel with warm boiled water; residual wax will float to the top and solidify for effortless removal.
