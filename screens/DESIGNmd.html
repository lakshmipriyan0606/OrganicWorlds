# Earth & Essence --- DESIGN.md ## Complete UI/UX Design Reference for Google Stitch **Document purpose:** This is the single source of truth for generating the complete visual design of the Earth & Essence organic lifestyle e-commerce website in Google Stitch. It is a design specification, not a production implementation plan. **Primary workflow:** Paste or upload this file in Google Stitch, generate the design in batches, review every screen, approve the design, and then provide the approved screens and this document to Antigravity AI Agent for implementation. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 1. Product Vision Design a premium, responsive, multi-page e-commerce experience for **Earth & Essence**, a temporary brand name for a natural lifestyle products business. ### Launch categories - Natural candles. - Hair oils and hair-care products. ### Future categories - Skincare and face care. - Handmade soaps. - Body care. - Essential oils and aromatherapy. - Bath and wellness products. - Gift hampers and bundles. - Seasonal collections. - Other natural lifestyle products. The design must be category-driven and extensible. Future categories must fit into the same visual system and reusable page templates without requiring a full redesign. ### Brand attributes - Botanical - Warm - Premium - Calm - Trustworthy - Thoughtful - Modern - Editorial - Accessible ### Audience Customers who appreciate natural lifestyle products, premium packaging, considered design, ingredient transparency, self-care rituals, home fragrance and thoughtful gifting. ### Market assumptions - Initial market: India. - Language: English. - Currency: Indian rupees (₹). - Brand name, logo, contact details, social links, policies and products are placeholders until confirmed. Do not invent certifications, verified organic claims, medical benefits, test results, sales statistics or authentic customer testimonials. Clearly treat sample products and sample prices as illustrative design content. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 2. Design-First Rules This assignment is for **design and frontend prototyping only**. Do not start production backend development. Do not create a real database, real authentication, payment processing, shipping integration or production APIs. Create actual visual screens and, where supported, a navigable interactive prototype. A written list of pages or a single homepage is not sufficient. Every page listed in this document must be: 1. Designed as an actual screen or explicitly marked as blocked by the tool. 2. Named consistently. 3. Connected to the correct navigation or user flow. 4. Based on the shared design system. 5. Considered for desktop and mobile behavior. 6. Included in a final screen inventory. If the platform cannot generate all screens in one operation, work through the batches in Section 13. Do not silently omit screens or claim that a screen is complete when only its name is documented. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 3. Visual Direction Create a high-fidelity premium botanical editorial e-commerce experience inspired by refined natural beauty, home fragrance and lifestyle brands. Use: - Editorial composition and generous whitespace. - Premium product and lifestyle photography. - Warm, natural materials and botanical details. - Elegant serif headlines. - Highly readable sans-serif body text. - Strong content hierarchy. - Clean product grids. - Minimal, purposeful borders. - Subtle interactions and restrained motion. - Consistent component geometry and spacing. Avoid: - Generic marketplace layouts. - Outdated 2019-style interfaces. - Excessive rounded cards. - Excessive shadows or gradients. - Neon colors and unnecessary glassmorphism. - Arbitrary font mixing. - Overly dense content. - Large empty areas without purpose. - Unrelated product imagery. - Decorative elements that compete with shopping tasks. - Unverified trust badges or fabricated reviews. The storefront should feel editorial and inviting. The admin dashboard should feel operational, clear and efficient while remaining part of the same brand. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 4. Layout, Spacing and Shape ### Responsive layout targets - Desktop artboard: 1440 px. - Laptop: 1280 px. - Tablet: 768 px. - Mobile: 390 px. - Maximum main content width: 1440 px. - Typical content width: 1200--1320 px. - Desktop side gutters: 64 px. - Tablet side gutters: 32 px. - Mobile side gutters: 20 px. ### Spacing tokens Use a 4 px base system: `4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 120 px`. Guidelines: - Desktop section spacing: 72--104 px. - Mobile section spacing: 48--64 px. - Product grid gap: 24 px desktop and 12--16 px mobile. - Form controls: approximately 48 px high. - Primary buttons: 48--52 px high. - Interactive touch targets: at least 44 × 44 px where practical. ### Shape and surfaces - Product cards: square or 4:5 image ratio. - Default card corners: 2--6 px. - Buttons: 3--6 px radius. - Inputs: 4--6 px radius. - Drawers and dialogs: 6--12 px radius. - Prefer fine borders over prominent shadows. - Avoid pill buttons except where the interaction requires them. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 7. Photography and Content Direction Use consistent, high-quality imagery with natural light and warm color grading. Preferred subjects: - Amber or frosted glass candle vessels. - Hair oil bottles and carefully arranged personal-care products. - Botanical ingredients and leaves. - Natural textures such as linen, stone, wood and ceramic. - Warm interiors and quiet daily rituals. - Editorial close-ups. - Thoughtful product packaging. - Gift arrangements and natural still life. ### Image ratios - Product cards: 4:5 or 1:1. - Product detail gallery: 4:5. - Desktop hero: wide landscape. - Mobile hero: portrait crop. - Editorial banner: wide landscape. - Category cards: consistent ratios within each collection. Do not stretch images. Do not use unrelated imagery to fill space. Use intentional image crops for each viewport. All example product data, prices, reviews and claims are placeholders. Do not present fake reviews, certifications or product efficacy claims as verified facts. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 8. Detailed Page Layout Specifications ## ST-01 --- Home Create a premium editorial homepage with these sections in order: 1. Announcement bar. 2. Main navigation. 3. Hero. 4. Category discovery. 5. Featured products. 6. Brand story. 7. Craft and ingredients. 8. Featured collection. 9. Gift discovery. 10. Newsletter. 11. Footer. ### Hero layout Desktop: two-column layout, text approximately 42% and image approximately 58%, with a visual height of about 600--680 px. Suggested heading: "Bring nature into your everyday." Supporting copy: "Thoughtfully chosen essentials for slower rituals, softer spaces and everyday care." Primary CTA: "Shop the Collection". Secondary CTA: "Discover Our Story". Use a high-quality candle and botanical lifestyle image. On mobile, stack the content intentionally, use a portrait image crop and keep the primary CTA easy to find. ### Category discovery Two large image-led cards: Candles and Hair Care. Each includes an image, title, short description and clear CTA. ### Featured products Four columns on desktop, three where appropriate on tablet, and two on mobile. Cards include image, optional badge, name, price, optional variant detail and wishlist control. ### Brand story Two-column image-and-copy section with an editorial heading and Our Story CTA. ### Craft and ingredients Use an image and three supporting themes, such as thoughtful selection, careful making and everyday rituals. Claims must be verified before launch. ### Featured collection and gifts Use a wide image-led campaign banner, followed by a curated gift section. ### Newsletter Use an ivory or sage panel with email input, consent note, subscribe CTA and success/error states. ## ST-02 --- Shop All Include breadcrumbs, page title, concise introduction, result count, sorting, filtering, active filter chips, product grid, pagination or load-more control, and an empty-results state. Desktop: optional 230--260 px filter sidebar and three- or four-column grid. Mobile: two-column grid where readable; Filter and Sort actions open accessible drawers or bottom sheets. Possible filters: category, price, availability, product type, fragrance or size when relevant. Do not show irrelevant filters. ## ST-03 --- All Categories Create a visual category directory with consistent image-led cards, titles, concise descriptions and Explore CTAs. Make it easy to add future categories. ## ST-04, ST-05 and ST-06 --- Category pages Use a reusable category template with category hero, title, introduction, optional subcategory navigation, filters, sorting, product grid, editorial content, related collections and an SEO content area. Provide empty-cart and unavailable-item states. ## ST-14 --- Wishlist Show saved product cards, prices, availability, remove action and add-to-cart action where appropriate.
